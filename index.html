Why this isn't hopeless

🌞 Clean power is winning on price. Most new solar and wind projects now produce electricity more cheaply than new fossil fuel plants (IRENA).
🛡️ We've fixed a sky problem before. The 1987 Montreal Protocol banned ozone-eating chemicals. The ozone layer is on track to recover by around 2066 over Antarctica (UNEP/WMO 2023).
📉 Rich countries can cut and grow. The UK roughly halved its territorial emissions since 1990 while its economy kept growing.
🌡️ Every bit counts. Warming roughly stops when CO₂ emissions reach net zero. Every tenth of a degree we avoid means fewer extreme heatwaves (IPCC AR6).
